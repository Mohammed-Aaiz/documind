4. SPAN ALIGNMENT (CRITICAL FIX)
======================================================================

Previous bug (4F.4):
  return_offsets_mapping was NOT requested, causing fallback heuristic
  to use character offsets as token indices. 99.3% of training labels
  were wrong.

Fix applied:
  1. Tokenizer called with return_offsets_mapping=True
  2. Search ONLY within context tokens (after first [SEP]) to avoid
     offset collision between question and context tokens
  3. Trailing whitespace in answers handled via trimmed answer_end

Results:
  Total answerable:     876
  Valid spans:          872 (99.5%)
  Invalid spans:          4 (0.5%)
    All 4 are genuine dataset annotation errors
    (offset misalignment in source JSONL)
  Truncated:             0
  Exact decoded match: 136 (15.5%)

The 15.5% exact decoded match rate is expected because:
  - distilbert-base-uncased lowercases all text
  - Subword tokenization may insert whitespace at boundaries
  - These are tokenizer artifacts, not alignment errors

Invalid examples (all in training set):
  tc_3b383990: span offset points past context end
  li_8bc7889f: answer_start off by 6 chars
  li_4fa4a839: answer_start off by 8 chars
  li_8770780b: answer_start off by 10 chars

These 4 are marked INVALID_SPAN and excluded from training.
Validation and test sets have 100% valid spans.
