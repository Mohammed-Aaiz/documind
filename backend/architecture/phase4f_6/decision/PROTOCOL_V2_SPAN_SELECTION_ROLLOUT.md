# Protocol v2 Rollout — replaying the Phase 4F runners under both span-selection rules

> The 10 Phase 4F runners were moved from their inline greedy argmax chain to the
> shared `answer_span.select_answer_span()`. That function defaults to the **joint**
> rule (`maximise start_logit + end_logit`) — the rule production already used —
> and `PHASE4F_SPAN_SELECTION=argmax_chain` restores the recorded v1 behaviour.
> This record replays the eval-only runners under **both** rules and reports what
> actually changed.
> **Every variant reads the same logits. Population 100% leaked — not a
> generalization result.**

## Why this replay was needed

The `.pre_joint_selection_v1` snapshots were taken from whatever was on disk. For
`phase4f_5/results` that turned out to be a **stale baseline**: the Sep-21 files are
byte-equal in their `overall` block to `results.pre_reconstruction_fix`, i.e. they
predate the Exp-1 answer-reconstruction fix. A v1 baseline therefore had to be
**re-measured**, not copied from disk.

Replaying with `argmax_chain` reproduces a *different* (higher) v1 number than the
Sep-21 file — because the reconstruction fix is now in place:

| model | Sep-21 on disk (pre-reconstruction-fix) | v1 re-run (`argmax_chain`) | v2 (`joint`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 (production) | EM 3.2 / F1 8.6 | EM 8.6 / F1 14.7 | **EM 13.1 / F1 29.6** |
| A1 (`checkpoint_epoch3`) | EM 1.8 / F1 14.4 | EM 11.3 / F1 29.9 | EM 11.3 / F1 29.3 |
| A2 (`checkpoint_epoch3`) | EM 0.9 / F1 12.3 | EM 10.9 / F1 27.5 | EM 10.9 / F1 27.0 |

`phase4f_5/evaluation/evaluate.py`, `derived_dataset.jsonl` split `test`, n=221.

v1 = `threshold_experiment.pre_joint_selection_v1/` (verified byte-identical to the live
directory immediately before the replay). v2 = fresh run.

|---|---|---|---|
| benchmark EM | 14.29% | 15.00% | **+0.71pp** |
| benchmark F1 | 28.91% | 29.69% | **+0.78pp** |
| `TABLE_CELL` EM / F1 | 4.17% / 12.08% | 8.33% / 16.25% | +4.16pp / +4.17pp |

Decision rule selected under both protocols: `null_score >= 1.5705`.

| A0 overall F1 | 18.9% | **38.7%** |
| A1 overall F1 | 35.7% | 35.3% |

## Findings

1. **`ROLL1` — joint helps A0 strongly (CONFIRMED).** +4.5pp EM / +14.9pp F1 on the
   221-example split; +7.8pp EM / +19.8pp F1 on the 182-example benchmark.
2. **`ROLL2` — joint is NOT uniformly better (CONFIRMED).** A1: −0.6pp F1 (split),
   −0.4pp F1 (benchmark). A2: −0.5pp F1. F2: +0.71pp EM on benchmark but −1.18pp EM
   on validation. The upgrade must not be described as a uniform improvement.
3. **`ROLL3` — joint changes a research conclusion (CONFIRMED).** Under v1, A1 looked
   clearly better than production A0 (+7.8pp EM, +16.8pp F1). Under v2 the EM
   advantage vanishes and A1 is *worse* on F1 (−3.4pp). Earlier "A1 beats A0" claims
   rest on the v1 selection rule.
4. **`ROLL4` — joint removes A0's accidental abstention (CONFIRMED).** A0
   `unanswerable_abstained` 27 → 0 while `answerable_correct` 11 → 22. Under v1 the
   greedy chain often produced an empty string, which was being read as abstention.
   That abstention was a selection-rule artifact, not calibrated behaviour.
5. **`ROLL5` — abstention calibration is protocol-stable (CONFIRMED).** The same
   threshold is selected under both rules and the unanswerable-only holdout is
   byte-identical. The protocol moves answerable quality, not abstention.

## Not replayed

| runner | reason |
|---|---|
| `phase4f/experiments/evaluate.py` (n=918) | The A0–A3 checkpoint directories it consumes are absent; only its output dirs exist. Cannot replay without inventing a model directory. |
| `phase4f_5d/build_benchmark.py` | Performs **no inference** (no `logits`/`argmax`/`torch`/`AutoModel`) and writes only dataset-shape audits, so it cannot change a protocol-dependent metric. It would, however, unconditionally rewrite the git-tracked, sha-pinned `qa_benchmark_v1.jsonl` (no checksum guard). **Requires hardening** before it can be safely re-run. |
| `phase4f_5d/analysis/{a0,a1,a2}_benchmark_v1.json`, `benchmark_comparison.json` | No producer script exists on disk; left untouched rather than regenerated by guesswork. Signal is covered by the `phase4f_5e` replay. |

## Verdict

`PROTOCOL_V2_ADOPTED_AS_DEFAULT_BUT_NOT_A_UNIFORM_IMPROVEMENT`

- v2 is already the rule production uses, so making it the runner default removes a
  research/serving divergence rather than adding a method.
- v1 remains reproducible via `PHASE4F_SPAN_SELECTION=argmax_chain`.
- The A1-vs-A0 comparison must be re-stated under v2.
- Gains are concentrated in A0 and `TABLE_CELL`.

## Limitations

- Population is 100% leaked (every `example_id` appears in the training data).
- One example ≈ 0.55pp on n=182 and ≈ 0.45pp on n=221: sub-1pp deltas are a single
  example and are not evidence of a real effect.
- Wall times (45–349s) are single observations, not benchmarks.
