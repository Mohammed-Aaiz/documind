================================================================================
14. LIMITATIONS
================================================================================
1. Some documents contribute fewer negatives than others due to
   limited eligible contexts after quality gate filtering.

2. MULTI_CHUNK_ABSENT_FACT category has only 2 examples in H30,
   which may limit the model's learning of cross-chunk reasoning.

3. The semantic audit is automated (context-based verification);
   human review would provide additional confidence.

4. All candidates use existing QA examples as context source;
   contexts are short chunks rather than full documents, which
   may not fully represent long-context unanswerable scenarios.

5. Question templates, while context-specific, follow patterns
   that could potentially be learned as artifacts by the model.

15. RECOMMENDED CANDIDATE POOL FOR H1
================================================================================
Recommendation: H20

Rationale:
  - 114 examples (20% of answerable training pool)
  - All 11 categories covered
  - 17/18 eligible documents represented
  - No document exceeds 20% concentration
  - 0 leakage into benchmark
  - 0 duplicate questions
  - Reasonable class balance
  - Superset of H10 for easy scaling

The H20 pool provides sufficient negative examples to test the
hypothesis that representative, document-grounded unanswerable
examples improve abstention behavior, without overwhelming the
answerable training signal.

H30 (171 examples) is also viable if the experiment shows H20
provides insufficient signal, but H20 is the recommended starting
point to avoid excessive negative-to-answerable ratio.

H10 (57 examples) may be too small to provide meaningful signal,
especially for underrepresented categories.
