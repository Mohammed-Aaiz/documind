============================================================
15. ANALYSIS
============================================================

The F2 experiment was designed to test whether adding 20
genuine unanswerable examples to training would teach
DistilBERT to abstain on unanswerable questions.

RESULT: The experiment FAILED to produce abstention.

Root cause: The unanswerable-to-answerable ratio (20:486 =
1:24.3) is too low. The model's gradient updates are
overwhelmingly driven by answerable examples, and the 20
unanswerable examples provide insufficient signal to learn
the abstention behavior.

The model converges on answerable QA (loss decreases from
5.71 to 3.82) but never learns to output no-answer for
unanswerable questions.

============================================================
16. DECISION
============================================================

F2_NO_MEANINGFUL_SIGNAL

Reasoning:
- Abstention rate: 0.0% across all evaluation sets
- No improvement over a model that never abstains
- The 20 unanswerable training examples are insufficient
- Answerable performance is also low (5.9% EM on validation)
- No evidence that targeted unanswerable training helps
  with the abstention failure identified in Phase 4F.5-E

The experiment confirms that simply adding a small number
of unanswerable examples to training is NOT sufficient to
teach abstention. A fundamentally different approach is
needed (e.g., much higher unanswerable ratio, different
loss function, or architectural changes).
